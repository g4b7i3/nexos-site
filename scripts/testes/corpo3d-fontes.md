# Corpo 3D: fontes dos textos das condições

Os textos do Corpo 3D (app/js/31zd-corpo3d-dados.js) são educativos e foram escritos a partir destas referências.
Ao mudar um texto, confira a fonte. Não invente número nem prevalência.

## Joelho
- Dor patelofemoral: Crossley KM et al. 2016 Patellofemoral pain consensus statement (5th International Patellofemoral Pain Research Retreat), Br J Sports Med 50:839-843. Willy RW et al. 2019 Patellofemoral Pain, Clinical Practice Guideline, J Orthop Sports Phys Ther 49(9):CPG1-CPG95 (inclui aumento de cadência).
- Banda iliotibial: Aderem J, Louw QA 2015 Biomechanical risk factors associated with iliotibial band syndrome in runners: a systematic review, BMC Musculoskelet Disord 16:356. Baker RL, Fredericson M 2016 Iliotibial Band Syndrome in Runners, Phys Med Rehabil Clin N Am 27(1):53-77.
- Tendinopatia patelar: Malliaras P et al. 2015 Patellar Tendinopathy: Clinical Diagnosis, Load Management, and Advice for Challenging Case Presentations, J Orthop Sports Phys Ther 45(11):887-898.

## Perna
- Estresse tibial medial: Winters M et al. 2013 Treatment of medial tibial stress syndrome: a systematic review, Sports Med 43(12):1315-1333. Winters M et al. 2018 Medial tibial stress syndrome can be diagnosed reliably using history and physical examination, Br J Sports Med 52:1267-1272.
- Tendinopatia de Aquiles: Martin RL et al. 2018 Achilles Pain, Stiffness, and Muscle Power Deficits: Midportion Achilles Tendinopathy Revision 2018, J Orthop Sports Phys Ther 48(5):A1-A38. Silbernagel KG et al. 2007 Continued sports activity, using a pain-monitoring model, during rehabilitation in patients with Achilles tendinopathy, Am J Sports Med 35(6):897-906.
- Lesão de panturrilha: Green B, Pizzari T 2017 Calf muscle strain injuries in sport: a systematic review of risk factors for injury, Br J Sports Med 51:1189-1194.

## Tornozelo e pé
- Dor plantar do calcanhar: Koc TA et al. 2023 Heel Pain – Plantar Fasciitis: Revision 2023, J Orthop Sports Phys Ther 53(12):CPG1-CPG39. Rathleff MS et al. 2015 High-load strength training improves outcome in patients with plantar fasciitis, Scand J Med Sci Sports 25(3):e292-e300.
- Entorse de tornozelo: Doherty C et al. 2017 Treatment and prevention of acute and recurrent ankle sprain: an overview of systematic reviews with meta-analysis, Br J Sports Med 51:113-125. Regras de Ottawa: Stiell IG et al. 1992/1993, Ann Emerg Med / JAMA.
- Fratura por estresse: Warden SJ et al. 2014 Management and prevention of bone stress injuries in long-distance runners, J Orthop Sports Phys Ther 44(10):749-765. Mountjoy M et al. 2023 IOC consensus statement on Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (REDs), Br J Sports Med 57:1073-1097.

## Coxa
- Posterior de coxa e flexão nórdica: van Dyk N et al. 2019 Including the Nordic hamstring exercise in injury prevention programmes halves the rate of hamstring injuries, Br J Sports Med 53:1362-1370.
- Virilha e prancha de Copenhague: Harøy J et al. 2019 The Adductor Strengthening Programme prevents groin problems among male football players, Br J Sports Med 53:150-157.

## Quadril
- Tendinopatia glútea: Mellor R et al. 2018 Education plus exercise versus corticosteroid injection use versus a wait and see approach on global outcome and pain from gluteal tendinopathy (LEAP), BMJ 361:k1662. Grimaldi A, Fearon A 2015 Gluteal Tendinopathy: Integrating Pathomechanics and Clinical Features in Its Management, J Orthop Sports Phys Ther 45(11):910-922.
- Fratura por estresse do colo do fêmur (sinal de alerta): Warden SJ et al. 2014 (acima).

## Lombar
- Dor lombar: Hartvigsen J et al. 2018 What low back pain is and why we need to pay attention, Lancet 391:2356-2367. Foster NE et al. 2018 Prevention and treatment of low back pain: evidence, challenges, and promising directions, Lancet 391:2368-2383. Hayden JA et al. 2021 Exercise therapy for chronic low back pain, Cochrane Database Syst Rev CD009790.
- Sinais de alerta: Finucane LM et al. 2020 International Framework for Red Flags for Potential Serious Spinal Pathologies, J Orthop Sports Phys Ther 50(7):350-372.

## Ombro
- Dor no ombro e manguito rotador: Littlewood C et al. 2015 Exercise for rotator cuff tendinopathy: a systematic review, Physiotherapy 101(2):101-110. Lähdeoja T et al. 2020 Subacromial decompression surgery for adults with shoulder pain: a systematic review with meta-analysis, Br J Sports Med 54:665-673 (cirurgia não foi melhor que exercício ou placebo).

## Braço e cotovelo
- Dor no lado de fora do cotovelo (epicondilalgia lateral): Coombes BK, Bisset L, Vicenzino B 2015 Management of lateral elbow tendinopathy: one size does not fit all, J Orthop Sports Phys Ther 45(11):938-949. Faixa de idade: Shiri R et al. 2006 Prevalence and determinants of lateral and medial epicondylitis: a population study, Am J Epidemiol 164(11):1065-1074 (maior prevalência dos 45 aos 54 anos).

## Regiões do resto do corpo
- Antebraço e mão, cabeça e rosto, pescoço, coluna inteira, coração e vasos, sistema nervoso, órgãos, esqueleto completo e músculos do corpo todo são só para explorar (sem condições). As funções de cada estrutura são descrições anatômicas básicas.
